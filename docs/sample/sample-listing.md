## Overview

**Project:** The Canopies at Yas Point
**Developer:** Aldar Properties
**Location:** Yas Island, Abu Dhabi
**Tagline:** *A rhythm of everyday life*

The first address at Yas Island's new master-planned destination — a collection of six buildings
within Yas Point, a AED 6 billion, 600,000 sqm master plan comprising roughly 1,600 homes and a
700 m retail promenade. Yas Island is home to Yas Marina Circuit (F1), Warner Bros. World, Ferrari
World, SeaWorld, Yas Links Golf Course, Etihad Arena, and Yas Mall.
